# DefectLens

Explainable industrial defect inspection: PatchCore (implemented from scratch) vs. a
Gabor + LBP classical baseline, trained only on defect-free MVTec AD images. Pick a product
category, inspect a test image or upload your own, and get a heatmap, defect boxes, a
PASS/FAIL report and nearest-normal-patch evidence.

Dataset: MVTec AD (Bergmann et al.), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial use only.
